RØDMYRLIA 39 AS is a private limited company (AS) based in Notodden, founded 2012-07-10, registered in the industry “Utleie av egen eller leid fast eiendom” (68.200). [5][7][11][12][14]

Its latest filed company accounts (period ending 2025-12-31) show revenue of NOK 7.9 m (+8% vs 2024, computed from filed figures), an operating result of NOK 5.0 m, an annual result of NOK 3.6 m, equity of NOK 12.3 m. [35][44][60][68][69]

Normalized accounts are available for 2023, 2024, 2025. [67][68][69]

Annual-account copies are on file for 14 years (2012–2025). [45]

Registered leadership: Arve Bakken (chair of the board); CEDRA NORGE AS (auditor); the board has 1 member. [83][85]
