Summary

ARKITEKTFIRMA JON VIKØREN AS is a private limited company (AS) based in Vik, founded 2003-02-05, registered in the industry “Arkitektvirksomhet” (71.110). [5][7][11][12][13][16]

Its verified website (https://www.arkjv.no/) describes the business as: “Powered by SmugMug” (self-reported). [88][90]

The register records its purpose/activity as: “Yta rådgjevande arkitektfaglege tenester. Selskapet kan også gjennom aksjekjøp eller på andre måtar engasjera seg i andre føretak uansett føremål og selskapsform.”. [24]

Its latest filed company accounts (period ending 2025-12-31) show revenue of NOK 4.2 m (-11% vs 2024, computed from filed figures), an operating result of NOK -127 k, an annual result of NOK 5 k, equity of NOK 3.1 m. [36][45][60][68][69]

Normalized accounts are available for 2023, 2024, 2025. [67][68][69]

Annual-account copies are on file for 15 years (2011–2025). [46]

Registered leadership: Lin Marie Vikøren (CEO (daglig leder)); Lin Marie Vikøren (chair of the board); the board has 2 members. [83][84]

It has 1 registered workplace in Vik. [82]
