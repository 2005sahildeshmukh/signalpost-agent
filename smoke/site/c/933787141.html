Summary

AIOTA AS is a private limited company (AS) based in Kristiansund, founded 2024-06-15, registered in the industry “Dataprogrammeringstjenester” (62.100). [5][7][11][12][14][16]

Its verified website (https://www.aiota.no/) describes the business as: “Aiota AS tilbyr en innovativ plattform for datafangst og analyse, som hjelper bedrifter med å optimalisere driften og ta datadrevne beslutninger.” (self-reported). [70][72]

The register records its purpose/activity as: “Selge varer og tjenester innen programmering og annet som naturlig sammenfaller med dette samt deltakelse i andre selskaper, investeringer og nærliggende virksomhet.”. [25]

Its latest filed company accounts (period ending 2025-12-31) show revenue of NOK 658 k (-29% vs 2024, computed from filed figures), an operating result of NOK -287 k, an annual result of NOK -281 k, equity of NOK 66 k. [32][38][48][52][53]

Normalized accounts are available for 2024, 2025. [52][53]

Annual-account copies are on file for 2 years (2024–2025). [39]

Registered leadership: Mikael Lervik Thorsgaard (CEO (daglig leder)); Mikael Lervik Thorsgaard (chair of the board); the board has 1 member. [63][64]

It has 1 registered workplace in Kristiansund. [62]
