Summary

DIFFER NORGE HOLDING AS is a private limited company (AS) based in Oslo, founded 2019-01-25, registered in the industry “Bedriftsrådgivning og annen administrativ rådgivning” (70.200). [6][8][12][13][14]

The register records its purpose/activity as: “Selv, eller gjennom hel- eller deleide selskaper, å drive virksomhet innenfor strategi, organisasjonsutvikling og omstilling, management consulting, utleie av personell samt annen dermed forenlig virksomhet.”. [23]

Its latest filed company accounts (period ending 2025-12-31) show revenue of NOK 11.0 m (+15% vs 2024, computed from filed figures), an operating result of NOK 81 k, an annual result of NOK 4.4 m, equity of NOK 101 k. [35][44][59][65][66]

Normalized accounts are available for 2023, 2024, 2025. [64][65][66]

Annual-account copies are on file for 7 years (2019–2025). [45]

Registered leadership: Svein Arne Lamo (CEO (daglig leder)); Lina Johanna Hed (chair of the board); CEDRA NORGE AS (auditor); the board has 2 members. [83][84][86]

It has 1 registered workplace in Oslo. [82]

Official group structure lists XLENT AB (924729910) as its parent; group facts are kept separate. [79]

It is registered as parent of 2 subsidiaries. [80][81]
