Summary

INSBO SIRNES FARGE & INTERIØR AS is a private limited company (AS) based in Flekkefjord, founded 1991-04-30, registered in the industry “Detaljhandel med jernvarer, byggevarer, fargevarer og glass” (47.520). [6][8][12][13][14]

Its latest filed company accounts (period ending 2025-12-31) show revenue of NOK 14.7 m (+16% vs 2024, computed from filed figures), an operating result of NOK 671 k, an annual result of NOK 390 k, equity of NOK 2.0 m. [36][45][60][68][69]

Normalized accounts are available for 2023, 2024, 2025. [67][68][69]

Annual-account copies are on file for 15 years (2011–2025). [46]

The register reports 9 employees. [19]
