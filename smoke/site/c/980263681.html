Summary

ÅSGREINA BYGGASSISTANSE AS is a private limited company (AS) based in Gjerdrum, founded 1998-10-26, registered in the industry “Oppføring av bygninger” (41.000). [4][6][10][11][13]

The register records its purpose/activity as: “Bygg- og entreprenørvirksomhet samt vedlikehold og annen virksomhet som står i forbindelse med dette samt deltagelse i andre selskaper.”. [22]

Its latest filed company accounts (period ending 2025-12-31) show revenue of NOK 4.4 m (+50% vs 2024, computed from filed figures), an operating result of NOK 232 k, an annual result of NOK 206 k, equity of NOK 534 k. [34][43][58][66][67]

Normalized accounts are available for 2023, 2024, 2025. [65][66][67]

Annual-account copies are on file for 15 years (2011–2025). [44]

Registered leadership: Leander Slåttum Vesterås (CEO (daglig leder)); Oliver Slåttum Vesterås (chair of the board); the board has 3 members. [81][82]

It has 1 registered workplace in Gjerdrum. [80]
