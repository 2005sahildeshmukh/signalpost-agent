Summary

WEDO AS is a private limited company (AS) based in Ringerike, founded 2015-01-19, registered in the industry “Undervisning innenfor idrett og rekreasjon” (85.510). [5][7][11][12][14]

The register records its purpose/activity as: “Trening, coaching, massasje og andre sports og skade relaterte behandlinger samt salg av sportsutstyr.”. [23]

Its latest filed company accounts (period ending 2025-12-31) show revenue of NOK 259 k (-23% vs 2024, computed from filed figures), an operating result of NOK 68 k, an annual result of NOK 53 k, equity of NOK 45 k. [34][43][57][65][66]

Normalized accounts are available for 2023, 2024, 2025. [64][65][66]

Annual-account copies are on file for 11 years (2015–2025). [44]

Registered leadership: Jesper Makholm-Fimreite (CEO (daglig leder)); Jesper Makholm-Fimreite (chair of the board); the board has 1 member. [80][81]

It has 1 registered workplace in Ringerike. [79]
