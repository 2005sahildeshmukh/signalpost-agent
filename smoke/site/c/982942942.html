Summary

BRANDMAKER AS (public brand “Brandmaker.no”) is a private limited company (AS) based in Bergen, founded 2000-12-28, registered in the industry “Grafisk design og visuell kommunikasjonsdesign” (74.120). [5][7][11][12][14][16]

Its verified website (https://brandmaker.no/) describes the business as: “Webdesign og Emballasjesdesign i Bergen. Brandmaker designer nettsider, 3D animasjoner, nettbutikk reklame og grafisk design.” (self-reported). [92][93]

The register records its purpose/activity as: “Reklamebyrå og annen næringsvirksomhet.”. [24]

Its latest filed company accounts (period ending 2025-12-31) show revenue of NOK 212 k (+11% vs 2024, computed from filed figures), an operating result of NOK 20 k, an annual result of NOK 15 k, equity of NOK 163 k. [36][45][61][69][70]

Normalized accounts are available for 2023, 2024, 2025. [68][69][70]

Annual-account copies are on file for 15 years (2011–2025). [46]

Registered leadership: Jarle Indrøy (CEO (daglig leder)); Jarle Indrøy (chair of the board); the board has 1 member. [85][86]

1 registered role holder(s) are also named on the company's own website. [83]

It has 1 registered workplace in Bergen. [84]
