Summary

AAS ELEKTRONIKK AS is a private limited company (AS) based in Arendal, founded 2005-08-09, registered in the industry “Annen teknisk konsulentvirksomhet” (71.129). [5][7][11][12][14][16]

Its verified website (http://www.aelektronikk.no/) describes the business as: “Aas Elektronikk AS tilbyr tjenester innenfor Mekatronikk, 3D engineering, produktutvikling.” (self-reported). [86][87]

The register records its purpose/activity as: “Teknisk kosulentvirksomhet samt en hver annen aktivitet som naturlig hører med til dette. Videre deltakelsen i andre selskaper.”. [25]

Its latest filed company accounts (period ending 2025-12-31) show revenue of NOK 1.4 m (+154% vs 2024, computed from filed figures), an operating result of NOK 196 k, an annual result of NOK 198 k, equity of NOK 175 k. [37][46][60][68][69]

Normalized accounts are available for 2023, 2024, 2025. [67][68][69]

Annual-account copies are on file for 15 years (2011–2025). [47]

Registered leadership: Tor Ivar Aas (CEO (daglig leder)); Tor Ivar Aas (chair of the board); the board has 1 member. [83][84]

It has 1 registered workplace in Arendal. [82]
