TOTO INDUSTRIER AS is a private limited company (AS) based in Bø, founded 2005-04-14, registered in the industry “Uoppgitt” (00.000). [6][8][12][13][15]

The register records its purpose/activity as: “Investering i- og omsetning av kapitalvarer, verdipapirer og andre virksomheter, herunder utvikling, utleie og investering i eiendom, samt forretningsorientert rådgivning og konsulenttjenester.”. [25]

Its latest filed company accounts (period ending 2025-12-31) show revenue of NOK 1.3 m (+1887% vs 2024, computed from filed figures), an operating result of NOK 228 k, an annual result of NOK 258 k, equity of NOK 1.8 m. [37][46][62][70][71]

Normalized accounts are available for 2023, 2024, 2025. [69][70][71]

Annual-account copies are on file for 15 years (2011–2025). [47]

Registered leadership: Linn Merete Torbjørnsen (CEO (daglig leder)); Torben Torbjørnsen (chair of the board); the board has 2 members. [85][86]

It has 1 registered workplace in Sortland. [84]
