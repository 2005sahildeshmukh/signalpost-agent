SKYA MARKEDSFØRING AS is a private limited company (AS) based in Tønsberg, founded 2020-11-22, registered in the industry “Reklamebyråvirksomhet” (73.110). [5][7][11][12][13]

Its latest filed company accounts (period ending 2025-12-31) show revenue of NOK 1.4 m (+9% vs 2024, computed from filed figures), an operating result of NOK 200 k, an annual result of NOK 198 k, equity of NOK -299 k. [35][44][59][67][68]

Normalized accounts are available for 2023, 2024, 2025. [66][67][68]

Annual-account copies are on file for 5 years (2021–2025). [45]

It has 1 registered workplace in Tønsberg. [81]
