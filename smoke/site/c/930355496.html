Summary

VINTAGE CAPITAL AS is a private limited company (AS) based in Oslo, founded 1963-12-19, registered in the industry “Eiendomsforvaltning på oppdrag” (68.320). [6][8][12][13][14]

The register records its purpose/activity as: “Handel og konsulentvirksomhet, eiendomsfor- valtning, investering i fast eiendom, samt aksjer og verdipapirer, herunder også deltagelse i andre selskaper.”. [25]

Its latest filed company accounts (period ending 2025-12-31) show revenue of NOK 27.0 m (-58% vs 2024, computed from filed figures), an operating result of NOK 24.7 m, an annual result of NOK 23.6 m, equity of NOK 458.0 m. [36][45][61][69][70]

Normalized accounts are available for 2023, 2024, 2025. [68][69][70]

Annual-account copies are on file for 15 years (2011–2025). [46]

Registered leadership: Christian Fredrik Furuholmen (CEO (daglig leder)); Øystein Bøe (chair of the board); CEDRA NORGE AS (auditor); the board has 3 members. [85][86][90]

It has 1 registered workplace in Oslo. [84]

It is registered as parent of 1 subsidiary. [83]
