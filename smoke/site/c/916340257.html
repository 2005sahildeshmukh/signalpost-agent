Summary

WYSSEN NORGE AS is a private limited company (AS) based in Sogndal, founded 2015-10-30, registered in the industry “Annen teknisk konsulentvirksomhet” (71.129). [4][6][10][11][12]

The register records its purpose/activity as: “(A) Produksjon, reparasjon, kjøp, salg, forsendelser, leasing, distribusjon, import, eksport og representasjon for følgende utstyr, reservedeler og tilbehør: (i) Utstyr for snøskredkontroll og andre naturfarer,…”. [21]

Its latest filed company accounts (period ending 2025-12-31) show revenue of NOK 39.2 m (+107% vs 2024, computed from filed figures), an operating result of NOK 4.1 m, an annual result of NOK 3.4 m, equity of NOK 6.2 m. [33][42][57][65][66]

Normalized accounts are available for 2023, 2024, 2025. [64][65][66]

Annual-account copies are on file for 11 years (2015–2025). [43]

Registered leadership: Stian Langeland (CEO (daglig leder)); Christian Wyssen (chair of the board); CEDRA NORGE AS (auditor); the board has 2 members. [81][82][85]

The register reports 5 employees. [16]

It has 2 registered workplaces in Sogndal, Tromsø. [79][80]

No active job ads with its organisation number were found in NAV's public job feed at retrieval time. [91]
