Summary

BUTIKKDRIFT UMAZABAL GUERRA AS is a private limited company (AS) based in Ås, founded 2025-03-21, registered in the industry “Detaljhandel med drivstoff til motorvogner” (47.300). [5][7][11][12][13]

The register records its purpose/activity as: “Drive butikkdrift med tilhørende virksomhet som utleie av maskiner og utstyr samt drive kioskhandel med bredt vareutvalg med hovedvekt på næring- og nytelsesmidler.”. [25]

Its latest filed company accounts (period ending 2025-12-31) show revenue of NOK 12.3 m, an operating result of NOK 708 k, an annual result of NOK 577 k, equity of NOK 601 k. [29][32][37][40]

Annual-account copies are on file for 1 years (2025–2025). [33]

Registered leadership: Yazmin Valeska Umazabal Guerra (CEO (daglig leder)); Yazmin Valeska Umazabal Guerra (chair of the board); PRICEWATERHOUSECOOPERS AS (auditor); the board has 1 member. [47][48][50]

The register reports 27 employees. [19]

It has 2 registered workplaces in Frogn, Ås. [45][46]

No active job ads with its organisation number were found in NAV's public job feed at retrieval time. [54]
